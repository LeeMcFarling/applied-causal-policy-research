# Guided Walkthroughs

These four draft case studies explain the reusable methods demonstrated by this repository. They are intended to become short, standalone PDFs for readers who may never browse the full file tree.

The policy examples are illustrative. The walkthroughs do not recommend that a future team adopt the underlying housing, wage, or regulatory proposals. They show how a team could structure its own work, test it against evidence, design evaluation into implementation, and make administrative processes more legible.

1. [Machine-Readable Policy Architecture](./01-machine-readable-policy-architecture.md)
2. [Research Integration and Adversarial Revision](./02-research-integration-and-adversarial-revision.md)
3. [Causal Evaluation Inside Policy Design](./03-causal-evaluation-inside-policy-design.md)
4. [GovOps and Process-Legible Law](./04-govops-and-process-legible-law.md)

Each walkthrough follows the same pattern:

- the problem;
- the demonstrated method;
- a worked example;
- what the example proves;
- what it does not prove; and
- what a future team would decide for itself.
