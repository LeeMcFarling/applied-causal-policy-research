# Guided Walkthroughs

These two case studies explain the reusable methods demonstrated by this repository. They are intended to become short, standalone PDFs for readers who may never browse the full file tree.

The policy examples are illustrative. The walkthroughs do not recommend that a future team adopt the underlying policy proposals. They show how a team could structure its own work and test it against evidence.
