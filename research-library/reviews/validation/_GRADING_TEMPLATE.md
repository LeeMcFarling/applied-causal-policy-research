## Purpose & Scope of This Grading Pass

*Read this before grading anything below.*

This pass grades the **fidelity** of the adversarial literature review — not the quality of the policy brief, not the rigor of the source literature, and not whether the review's design decisions were the right call. Judging the brief and the literature is the review's own job (its Aligned Findings / Gaps / Divergences / Design Decisions sections). This pass asks only:

1. Does the review accurately represent what the source material and the brief(s) actually say — does it get anything wrong, overstate it, or misrepresent it?
2. Did the review follow the process `_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md` requires — structurally and in citation discipline (page numbers accurate to the RAG chunks they were drawn from)?
3. Did the review cover the material it should have? A source can run 150 pages; the review only cites a fraction of it. This section exists to independently sample the uncited remainder and check whether the review missed a chapter or passage that aligns with, or complicates, the brief.

**Do not** re-litigate whether a Divergence's platform position is wise, whether a Gap matters strategically, or whether a Design Decision was the right call on the merits. Those are substantive judgments the review itself owns, and grading them here would conflate "faithful review" with "review I agree with." Grade only whether the review characterizes the underlying material correctly and completely. The human reviewer decides what changes, if any, follow from this report.
