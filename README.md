# Applied Causal Policy Research

A collection of methodologies, tools, and research frameworks for building evidence-based policy at scale.

Each project in this repository represents a distinct applied research effort — sharing a commitment to causal rigor, structured documentation, and institutional legibility, but addressing different domains and operating at different levels of the policy stack.

---

## Projects

### [`futures-project/`](./futures-project/)
A structured methodology for developing large-scale policy platforms from first principles. The Futures Project system applies phase-gated maturity tracking, YAML-based dependency management, adversarial research integration, and LLM-assisted validation to the problem of building policy documentation that is simultaneously rigorous, internally consistent, and institutionally legible.

**What it is:** A documentation architecture and research workflow, not a specific policy platform.  
**What it demonstrates:** How to build, track, and validate complex policy research across dozens of interdependent documents.

---

### [`govops/`](./govops/)
An engineering framework for the continuous optimization of public administration, adapting DevOps maintenance disciplines — version control, dependency tracking, operational observability, and controlled experimentation — to regulatory systems.

**What it is:** A research proposal for treating regulatory architecture as maintainable computational infrastructure.  
**What it demonstrates:** A dual-schema architecture (legal + workflow), cross-jurisdictional regulatory comparison, Regulatory Modernization Packets, and evidence-based regulatory deployment.

## Shared Principles

All projects in this repository are organized around the same core commitments:

- **Causal specificity over correlation** — claims require mechanism identification, not pattern-matching
- **Structured documentation** — every claim, dependency, and design decision is machine-readable and traceable
- **Adversarial review** — research integration is validated against the original source, not just summarized
- **Phase discipline** — documents advance through defined maturity gates with explicit gate conditions
- **Institutional legibility** — outputs are designed to be read by policymakers, researchers, and practitioners, not only specialists
